What BrikStax is

BrikStax is a free personal LEGO® collection tracker: it helps you catalog sets you own, track their estimated market value, and includes an optional gamified avatar/cosmetics system and an optional Community photo feature. BrikStax does not currently sell anything or offer in-app purchases.

No account required
