What's inside

Built for people who actually own the bricks.

A handful of things BrikStax does well, instead of a hundred it does halfway.

📦

Track every set

Scan a barcode or type a set number — sealed or open, purchase price, condition notes, quantity. Your whole collection, organized the way collectors actually think about it.

📈

Know what it's worth

Live pricing pulled from eBay, Rebrickable, and BrickSet, cached and refreshed automatically. See market value, unrealized gain, and ROI per set — not guesses, real comparables.

🧱

Build your minifig

Check in daily, complete hidden theme challenges, and unlock cosmetics for your own avatar and Brick Den display case. Collecting bricks, gamified — not gimmicky.

📸

Show off your shelf

Share a photo of your setup to the Community feed. Every submission is reviewed by a real person before it goes live, and location data is stripped automatically before upload.

🧩

Find what's missing

Load any set and check off the pieces you already have. Track partial builds across three save slots and export a shopping list of exactly what's left — a free tool, no app required.
📋

Merge sets into one shopping list

Building several sets at once? Enter every set number and get one combined parts list with quantities summed across all of them — ready to import into BrickLink. Also free, no app required.
